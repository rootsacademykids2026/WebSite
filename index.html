The programme

Faith, body and character — taught as one programme.

Every student moves through all three pathways every week. Sport isn't a break from the learning; it's how a lot of the learning happens.

  • Leadership and mentoring at Level 3

Why Roots

Five things that only work together.

Each of these stands on its own. Together, they're what makes a Saturday here different from a regular classroom.

Whole-child design

Faith, knowledge, body, character and belonging in one programme, not five separate commitments.

A real school building

Planned delivery in a Mississauga high school: proper classrooms and a full gym on one site.

Qualified educators

Teachers with years of classroom experience in Canada. Qur'an instruction includes huffaz and teachers holding ijazah.

Credible sports pathways

Professional academies and licensed coaches, plus a planned partnership with an internationally recognised Taekwondo academy for formal belt progression.

Built on what families said

Questions

Before you ask.

Where exactly is the school?

We run from a high-school site in Mississauga, chosen so that classrooms, a gym and prayer space all sit on the same campus. The full address and parking instructions go out to families once enrolment is confirmed.

We live in Milton or Oakville — is it practical for us?

Yes. The Mississauga location was picked partly because it's a reasonable drive for families in Milton, Oakville and west Mississauga. Because everything happens in one building on one day, it's a single drop-off rather than several trips across the week.

Is there parking and a drop-off area?

The site has a school car park with a coordinated drop-off and pick-up flow at the start and end of the day. We'll share the exact arrangements before the first session.

Which day and what are the hours?

One day each weekend, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, including breaks and congregational prayer. The confirmed day is listed on the registration form.

Who can attend?

Boys and girls ages 7 to 15, grouped into three levels: 7–9, 10–12 and 13–15.

Does my child need sports experience?

No. Sessions are run by coaches used to mixed abilities, and the point is participation and teamwork, not selection.

Does my child need previous Qur'an study?

No. Students are taught at their level, from beginning readers through to memorisation and tadabbur for older students.

What should we bring?

Comfortable clothing suitable for sport and prayer, running shoes, a water bottle and lunch. Martial arts uniform details are shared after enrolment.
